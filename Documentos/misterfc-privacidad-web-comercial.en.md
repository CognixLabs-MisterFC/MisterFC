### 17.3 Analytics on the information pages

The information pages of `misterfc.es` use Vercel Web Analytics. This tool does not set cookies or store identifiers in the browser, and it provides aggregated statistics about visits, such as the page visited, the referring site, the country and the type of device and browser. These analytics are not used in the MisterFC application.

The purpose is to understand how the information pages are used in order to improve them. To the extent that this processing involves personal data, its legal basis is the legitimate interest of Cognix Labs, S.L.U. in understanding how its website works and is used, under Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR. This processing is limited to the statistical purpose described, provides only aggregated statistics and is subject to the safeguards needed to protect the rights and freedoms of visitors, who can object to it by writing to info.cognixlabs@gmail.com.

Vercel acts as the provider of the tool. International transfers are governed by the previous section.

They can also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, AEPD) if they consider that the processing of their data does not comply with the regulations.
