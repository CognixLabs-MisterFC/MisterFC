# Cookie policy — MisterFC

Last updated: 16 October 2026

---

## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website may store in the user's browser or device to remember information, keep certain functions working or save preferences.

## 2. Cookies used

MisterFC uses its own technical or functional cookies. No advertising or profiling cookies are used. The website uses the following cookies:

| Cookie                                                                    | Purpose                                                                | Duration                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| `sb-<reference>-auth-token` and `sb-<reference>-auth-token-code-verifier` | Keep the user authenticated and their session open in the application. | 400 days                                                    |
| `NEXT_LOCALE`                                                             | Remember the selected language.                                        | Session, or 1 year if the language is chosen in the profile |
| `active_club_id`                                                          | Remember the active club for accounts linked to more than one club.    | 1 year                                                      |
| `active_player_id`                                                        | Remember the selected player for family or follower accounts.          | 1 year                                                      |
| `mfc_sidebar_collapsed`                                                   | Remember whether the application's side menu is collapsed.             | 1 year                                                      |

## 3. Difference between the public website and the application

On the public information pages (home, features, security and demonstration request), people who have not signed in receive only the `NEXT_LOCALE` cookie, to remember the language. The other cookies in the table are used when accessing the app.

## 4. Visitor analytics

The information pages use Vercel Web Analytics to obtain aggregated visit statistics. This tool does not set cookies or store identifiers in the browser, and it is used only on the information pages, not in the application. The purpose and legal basis of this processing are explained in the [Privacy policy](/en/legal/privacidad).

## 5. Information about consent

The website does not show a cookie consent banner because the cookies used are technical or functional and no advertising, profiling or third-party cookies are used.

## 6. How to manage cookies

Users can block or delete cookies in their browser settings. If they block cookies needed for authentication or for the application to work, some functions —including signing in— may stop working properly.

## 7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the cookies or technologies used change. The current version will be published on this same page and will show the date of its last update.
